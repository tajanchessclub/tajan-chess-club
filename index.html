About TCC

Our Chess Club started after the School Chess League last academic year. Even after the league ended, students continued bringing chess boards to school and playing during breaks. What started as a few students playing soon grew into a small chess community, eventually becoming the Chess Club.

From the beginning, we started thinking about how to grow the community, organize the club, and create a website that could bring everything together. After developing the idea throughout the year, we are now preparing to launch the website and take the club to its next stage.
